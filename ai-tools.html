How to choose an AI tool

Start with the task you need to complete rather than choosing a tool just because it is popular. Compare output quality, ease of use, privacy options, usage limits and the time needed to learn it.

Check before you sign up

  • Review the provider's current pricing and free-plan limits.
  • Read the privacy policy before uploading personal or confidential information.
  • Verify important facts instead of relying on AI output alone.
  • Test the tool with your own task before deciding whether it suits you.
