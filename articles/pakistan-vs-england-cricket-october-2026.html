Pakistan vs England Cricket: October 2026 ODI Tri-Series Guide

By Global Insight Editorial Desk

Pakistan and England meet in the October 2026 ODI tri-series in Pakistan, alongside Sri Lanka. Here are the announced fixtures, venues and official schedule links.

What is happening?

Pakistan is hosting England and Sri Lanka in an ODI tri-series scheduled for 18–31 October 2026. The announced schedule includes Pakistan versus England at Rawalpindi Cricket Stadium on 20 October and another Pakistan–England match at Gaddafi Stadium, Lahore, on 25 October. The final is scheduled for 31 October in Lahore, subject to the tournament schedule and any official changes.

The Pakistan Cricket Board (PCB) announced the tour dates, and the England and Wales Cricket Board (ECB) published the match-by-match schedule. Use the official schedule pages for the latest fixture status, start times and any changes rather than relying on reposted graphics.

Why this format matters

A tri-series gives each side opportunities to play more than one opponent before the final. In one-day international cricket, teams balance early wickets and scoring tempo with the need to preserve wickets for the closing overs. Bowlers may focus on controlling the run rate, while batters need to adapt to the pitch, boundary size and match situation.

Fixtures to note

  • 20 October: Pakistan vs England — Rawalpindi Cricket Stadium.
  • 25 October: Pakistan vs England — Gaddafi Stadium, Lahore.
  • 31 October: Tri-series final — Gaddafi Stadium, Lahore, as scheduled by the boards.

These are the announced fixtures, not a prediction of results. Match outcomes, playing XIs and live scores should be checked with official cricket sources on the day.
