Petrol Price in Pakistan on 30 October 2026: Latest Official Rates

By Global Insight Editorial Team

Pakistan State Oil lists petrol at Rs 377.91 per litre and high-speed diesel at Rs 400.67 per litre, effective 30 October 2026. Check the dated official sources before relying on a fuel-price figure.

Latest notified petrol price

Pakistan State Oil's published fuel-price page lists petrol (Premier Euro 5) at Rs 377.91 per litre and high-speed diesel at Rs 400.67 per litre, effective 30 October 2026. These are date-specific published rates; fuel prices can change through later government notifications.

The Oil and Gas Regulatory Authority (OGRA) also lists price publications for 30 October 2026. For the latest revision, readers should check the effective date on the official publication rather than relying on an older social-media graphic or a previously published article.

For context, the petrol price was Rs 381.14 per litre on 20 October 2026. The difference between that earlier rate and the 30 October rate illustrates why a fuel-price article must show the date attached to every figure.

Where to verify the current rate

Use the Pakistan State Oil fuel-prices page for its published retail rates and the OGRA Price Publications portal for dated price publications. The Ministry of Energy (Petroleum Division) publishes government notices and related updates.

Why petrol prices change

Fuel pricing can be affected by international petroleum prices, the rupee's exchange rate, freight and distribution costs, and government levies or other policy decisions. A change in the notified price does not always translate into the same change in every household's monthly spending, because fuel use varies by vehicle, distance and driving conditions.

How to verify a new rate

  1. Open the latest Petroleum Division notice or OGRA price publication.
  2. Check the effective date and the product name (petrol and high-speed diesel are different products).
  3. Compare the stated price per litre with a reputable news report, especially when a notification has just been issued.

What this means for drivers

A lower petrol rate can reduce the direct cost of refuelling, but transport fares and the prices of goods may respond at different speeds. Avoid relying on undated social-media graphics, as they can continue circulating after a new notification takes effect.

Sources

  • Pakistan State Oil — published fuel prices, effective 30 October 2026
  • OGRA — dated price publications
  • Ministry of Energy (Petroleum Division) — official notices
