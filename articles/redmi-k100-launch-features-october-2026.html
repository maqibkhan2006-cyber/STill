Redmi K100: Confirmed Features, Launch Date and What Is Still Unknown

Redmi has announced the K100's China launch for 27 October 2026 and revealed key display and gaming features. Price, full camera details and international availability still need confirmation.

Launch date and market availability

Redmi has announced that the standard Redmi K100 is scheduled to launch in China on 27 October 2026. As of 20 October, the announcement concerns the China launch. An international release date, including availability in Pakistan, should not be assumed until Xiaomi or Redmi publishes a specific regional announcement.

Features announced so far

Recent reporting based on Redmi's announcements says the phone is expected to have a 6.83-inch display with a refresh rate up to 165Hz, support native 165-frames-per-second mode in more than 40 games, and use a Snapdragon 8 Elite processor with a dedicated D2 gaming/display chip. Reports also describe a green colour option and Bose-tuned audio branding.

These details should be distinguished from unconfirmed claims circulating online. A high refresh rate can make supported motion appear smoother, but the actual gaming experience also depends on each game's support, temperature, power use and software optimisation.

What has not been confirmed here?

Do not treat an estimated price, battery capacity, camera specifications, Pakistan launch date or local warranty as official unless Xiaomi/Redmi publishes them for the relevant market. Third-party reports may be useful for context, but leaks and expected prices are not a substitute for a manufacturer specification sheet.

What buyers in Pakistan should check

  • Wait for a regional launch announcement and official local pricing.
  • Confirm PTA/DIRBS status, warranty coverage and the exact model number before buying an imported phone.
  • Compare the official specification sheet with the seller's listing, especially for RAM/storage and network-band support.
