Redmi K100 Launched in China: Price, Specifications and Availability

By Global Insight Editorial Desk

Redmi K100 launched in China on 27 October 2026 with a 165Hz display, Snapdragon 8 Elite chipset and 10,000mAh battery. Here are the reported China prices and the details still unconfirmed for Pakistan.

Launch date and China pricing

Xiaomi's Redmi K100 was launched in China on 27 October 2026. Reporting published after the launch lists three memory configurations: 12GB RAM with 256GB storage at CNY 3,499; 12GB with 512GB at CNY 4,099; and 16GB with 512GB at CNY 4,499. These are China-market prices, not confirmed prices for Pakistan or other regions.

The phone was reported as available for pre-order in China, with general sales scheduled for 31 October. Launch promotions and local subsidy prices may differ from the standard recommended retail prices and may not apply outside China.

Display and performance

The Redmi K100 has a 6.83-inch 1.5K AMOLED display with a refresh rate of up to 165Hz. A higher refresh rate can make supported scrolling and motion appear smoother, although the benefit depends on the app or game and can increase power use. The device is powered by Qualcomm's Snapdragon 8 Elite chipset and includes a dedicated D2 gaming chip, according to launch coverage.

Reported configurations include LPDDR5X memory and UFS 4.1 storage. These specifications describe the China model reported at launch; buyers should check the exact regional model before relying on a specification list from another market.

Battery, cameras and other features

Launch reports list a 10,000mAh battery, 100W wired charging and 27W reverse wired charging. The rear camera system is reported to include a 50-megapixel main camera with optical image stabilisation and an 8-megapixel ultra-wide camera. A 20-megapixel front camera is also listed in published specifications.

Coverage also reports an in-display fingerprint reader, 5G connectivity and high-level dust and water resistance ratings. These features should be checked against the manufacturer's regional specification sheet because configurations, network bands and certifications can vary by market.

What is confirmed for Pakistan?

The launch coverage cited below describes the China release. It does not establish a Pakistan launch date, local retail price, warranty terms or PTA approval status for imported units. Those details should only be treated as confirmed when Xiaomi publishes information for Pakistan or an authorised local distributor provides verifiable details.

What buyers in Pakistan should check

  • Wait for a regional launch announcement and official local pricing.
  • Confirm PTA/DIRBS status, warranty coverage and the exact model number before buying an imported phone.
  • Compare the official specification sheet with the seller's listing, especially for RAM/storage and network-band support.
