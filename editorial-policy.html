Editorial Policy

Global Insight aims to publish clear, accurate and useful reporting and explainers about technology, business, science, global developments and digital culture. This page describes the standards we use when preparing and maintaining content.

Research and sourcing

Articles should be based on identifiable sources appropriate to the subject. For developing news, we aim to link to original announcements, official documents or established news organisations. Readers should be able to distinguish our explanation from the source material.

Originality and value

We write our own summaries, context and explanations rather than copying or republishing another publisher's article. Quotes, facts and data from external sources should be attributed and linked. We do not treat a headline rewrite as a complete article.

Accuracy and updates

We aim to check names, dates, figures and claims before publication. News can change quickly, so articles may be updated when material information becomes available. When a meaningful update changes the story, the page should show an updated date and explain the change.

Corrections

If you believe an article contains a factual error, contact us through the website's Contact section and include the article URL and the detail that needs review. Confirmed material errors should be corrected transparently where appropriate.

Authorship and review

Bylines identify the author or editorial desk responsible for a page. We do not claim specialist credentials unless they are verified and relevant. Technical, financial and health-related explainers are general information and should not be treated as individual professional advice.

Advertising and independence

Advertising, if displayed, should be distinguishable from editorial content. Advertisers do not determine our editorial conclusions. Sponsored material, if published in future, should be clearly labelled.

Use of AI tools

Digital tools may assist research, editing or production. They do not remove the need for human fact-checking, source review and editorial responsibility. We aim not to publish unverified automated output as established fact.

Contact and feedback

For corrections, source suggestions or editorial feedback, use the Contact section on our website.
