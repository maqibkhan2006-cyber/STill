01. Gold Trading Basics

What does XAUUSD mean?

XAU is the market code commonly used for one troy ounce of gold, while USD is the US dollar. XAUUSD quotes show how many US dollars are needed for the quoted amount of gold. Check your broker's contract specifications.

What is a pip in gold?

Gold brokers do not all define a pip or minimum price increment in the same way. Check your platform's quote precision, tick size and contract specifications before calculating gains or losses.

What are spread and leverage?

The spread is the difference between a market's bid and ask prices. Leverage lets you control a larger position relative to your margin, but it also magnifies losses and can trigger liquidation.

What are trading sessions?

Gold can be traded across several major global market sessions. Liquidity, spreads and volatility can change throughout the day and around major economic announcements.

02. Technical Analysis

Support and resistance

Support and resistance are areas where price has previously struggled to move lower or higher. They are analytical zones, not guaranteed turning points.

Trend and market structure

Traders study successive highs and lows to describe possible uptrends, downtrends or ranges. A trend can change unexpectedly, so analysis should include invalidation conditions.

Candlestick patterns

Candlesticks summarise open, high, low and close prices over a chosen period. Individual patterns can be misleading when separated from broader context and risk controls.

Fundamental vs technical analysis

Fundamental analysis considers economic conditions and events. Technical analysis examines price and volume data. Neither method guarantees a reliable forecast, and they may give conflicting signals.

How to research a market headline

  1. Open the original release and check its publication date, time zone and methodology.
  2. Separate the reported figure from forecasts, commentary and social-media interpretations.
  3. Check whether other data or policy news was released at the same time.
  4. Record uncertainty: one indicator cannot explain every price movement.

Fundamentals

How US Inflation Data Can Influence Gold

Learn why inflation reports matter, how markets compare results with expectations, and why gold's response can differ from one release to another.

Inflation reports, including the Consumer Price Index, can change expectations for future interest-rate decisions. A report above or below expectations may influence bond yields and the US dollar, which can affect gold.

However, the market response also depends on the details of the report, earlier positioning and other simultaneous events. A single number is not a reliable trading signal.

Risk management

Understanding Risk Before Trading Gold

Discover how position size, stop-loss orders, spreads and leverage can affect potential losses on a gold position.

Before considering a trade, understand your broker's contract size, tick value, margin requirements and trading costs. Different account types can have different specifications.

A stop-loss order may not execute at the exact requested price during a fast market or a price gap. Leverage can lead to rapid losses, including the loss of all funds in a trading account.

Never use money needed for essential expenses, and consider practising with a demo account before risking real money.

Technical analysis

A Beginner's Guide to Support and Resistance

Understand price zones, breakouts, false breaks and why technical levels should be treated as uncertain rather than guaranteed.

Support is an area where buying interest has previously appeared; resistance is an area where selling pressure has previously appeared. These are interpretations of past price behaviour, not fixed boundaries.

A breakout can continue, fail or reverse. Traders often assess the broader trend, timeframe, market conditions and possible invalidation points instead of relying on one line.

Technical analysis is a framework for organising observations, not a guarantee that a market will move in a particular direction.

Protect your capital

Risk Management First

Understanding risk is an essential part of learning about leveraged markets.

⚠ Gold and leveraged trading involve substantial risk.

Consider these general educational principles before making any financial decision:

  • Learn contract size, margin, spread and tick value before trading.
  • Understand that leverage magnifies losses as well as gains.
  • Decide in advance how much risk you can afford to take.
  • Do not assume stop-loss orders guarantee an exact exit price.
  • Avoid trading decisions based only on social-media signals.
  • Never risk borrowed money or funds needed for essential expenses.
